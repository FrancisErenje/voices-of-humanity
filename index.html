MEET THE FOUNDER

Francis Odey Erenje

Founder & Executive Producer

I have always believed that every language deserves to be remembered. Behind every word lies a people's history, wisdom, traditions and identity. Yet every year, languages disappear forever, taking with them unique ways of understanding the world.

Voices of Humanity was born from a simple conviction: no language should disappear without leaving its story behind.

Through documentary filmmaking, research, education and digital preservation, this project seeks to build a permanent digital museum where every language, no matter how small, can be documented, preserved and celebrated for future generations.

"When we preserve a language, we preserve humanity itself."
