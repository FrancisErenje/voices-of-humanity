The Akpes people are among Nigeria's indigenous communities with a unique linguistic and cultural heritage. Their language reflects centuries of history, identity and traditional knowledge.

Although the language faces challenges today, community members and researchers continue working to preserve it for future generations.

About the Akpes Language

Akpes is one of Nigeria's endangered indigenous languages. It is spoken primarily in Akoko region of Ondo State. The language is known for its rich cultural traditions and unique linguistic heritage.

Where is it Spoken?

Akpes is spoken in several communities in Ondo State, Nigeria. Although the number of speakers has declined over time, efforts are being made to preserve the language.

Why It Matters

Every language carries history, identity and knowledge. Preserving Akpes helps preserve an irreplaceable part of Nigeria's cultural heritage.
