About Voices of Humanity

Preserving Languages. Documenting Cultures.

Every Meaningful Journey Begins with a Question.

What began as curiosity about the natural world eventually became a lifelong commitment to understanding humanity.

The journey started unexpectedly. After encountering a termite queen in my local community, I became fascinated by the remarkable organization of termite colonies. I researched their social structure and produced a documentary telling the story of their kingdom. But that experience raised a much bigger question.

If tiny insects could build such organized societies, how had human civilizations developed throughout history? Searching for that answer led me to something even more fascinating: language. Because every language tells the story of a people.

On 16 May 2026, I founded Voices of Humanity, a documentary project dedicated to researching, documenting and preserving Nigeria's indigenous languages and the cultures they represent. Today, the project continues to grow with documentaries, field research, photography and educational initiatives designed to preserve cultural heritage for future generations.

Meet the Founder

My name is Francis Erenje, Founder of LocalMedia247 and creator of Voices of Humanity.

I am an independent documentary researcher and producer dedicated to preserving indigenous languages, documenting cultures and building a lasting digital archive of humanity's diverse heritage.

My work combines documentary filmmaking, historical research, photography and community engagement to ensure that stories, traditions and languages are preserved for future generations.

Beyond documentary production, I actively contribute to language preservation by producing structured Igede language lessons with my father, a retired secondary school principal whose decades of teaching experience continue to inspire new learners online.

I believe that every language represents a unique way of understanding the world, and preserving those voices is one of the greatest gifts we can leave to future generations.

Our Philosophy

We believe that every language is a living library.

When a language disappears, humanity loses more than words. We lose songs that may never be sung again. Stories that may never be told again. Traditional knowledge that may never be rediscovered. Ways of understanding the world that may never return.

Voices of Humanity exists to ensure that these voices continue to speak. Through research, documentary filmmaking, photography and education, we are building a lasting archive for present and future generations.

We believe preserving humanity's diversity today helps preserve humanity's wisdom tomorrow.

Our Mission

To document, preserve and celebrate indigenous languages, cultures and oral histories through truthful documentary storytelling, ensuring that future generations inherit a richer understanding of humanity.

Our Vision

To become one of the world's leading independent documentary institutions for language preservation, cultural heritage and historical storytelling, beginning in Nigeria and expanding wherever humanity's stories deserve to be remembered.

Our Guiding Principles

✓ Truth before popularity.

✓ Respect every culture.

✓ Research before publication.

✓ Preserve before it disappears.

✓ Excellence in every documentary.

✓ Leave future generations something worth inheriting.
