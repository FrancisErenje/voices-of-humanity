01 / ABOUT VOICES OF HUMANITY

Document. Preserve. Share.

Voices of Humanity is an independent documentary initiative dedicated to documenting languages, preserving cultural knowledge and making underrepresented voices more visible.

The initiative is built on the belief that every language carries a history, every culture carries wisdom, and every voice matters.

To date, Voices of Humanity has successfully completed 30 language documentary projects, demonstrating a sustained commitment to independent linguistic and cultural documentation.

02 / THE NEXT DOCUMENTARY

Fam — Documentary No. 31

Documentary No. 31 is proposed to focus on the Fam language and its speakers in Nigeria. The project will explore the language, its speakers, cultural knowledge and place within Nigeria's linguistic heritage.

The project is currently at the preliminary research and preparation stage. The researcher has not yet visited the Fam-speaking community. The precise field location, appropriate community contacts and access arrangements must therefore be verified and established before fieldwork begins.

The initial phase will prioritise preliminary research, identification of the appropriate community, establishment of local contacts, respectful community engagement and assessment of fieldwork feasibility.

03 / PROJECT OBJECTIVES

What the project aims to achieve

  1. Conduct preliminary research to verify relevant linguistic information and identify the appropriate Fam-speaking community.
  2. Establish contact with community representatives and discuss access, participation and local arrangements.
  3. Undertake preliminary engagement to understand the community's preferences and expectations.
  4. Document selected language expressions, oral accounts and cultural knowledge, subject to informed consent and community participation.
  5. Produce a documentary that makes the community's voices and knowledge accessible to a wider audience.
  6. Preserve the completed documentary as part of the Voices of Humanity digital archive.

06 / ACCOUNTABILITY & SPONSOR RECOGNITION

Responsible support. Transparent reporting.

Voices of Humanity is committed to the responsible use of all contributions received.

Contributions and project expenditure will be properly recorded. Sponsors will receive appropriate updates on research progress, field activities and documentary production, together with a summary of how their contributions were utilised.

With the sponsor's agreement, acknowledgement can be arranged in a manner suitable to their preference. This may include:

  • Recognition in the documentary credits.
  • Acknowledgement in accompanying project materials.
  • Recognition as a documentary supporter or sponsor on appropriate Voices of Humanity platforms.
  • Private acknowledgement where the sponsor prefers not to be publicly identified.

No public acknowledgement will be made without the sponsor's consent.

If community access cannot be secured, or preliminary research reveals a significant feasibility issue, the sponsor will be informed before further expenditure or any proposed change in project direction. Any unspent funds or proposed reallocation will be discussed with the sponsor.
