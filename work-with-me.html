I am a Nigeria-based researcher and communications practitioner with experience across independent media, documentary production, editorial work and digital publishing. My work combines research, verification, clear writing and practical project delivery.

I founded LocalMedia247 in 2021 and Voices of Humanity in 2026. Through these initiatives, I have developed practical experience researching and producing content, checking information, documenting activities, managing digital publications and presenting complex subjects for public audiences.

I hold a Bachelor of Science degree in Chemistry from Benue State University. I bring an analytical approach to research alongside hands-on experience in writing, editing, multimedia production and online content management.
